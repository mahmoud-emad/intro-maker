# Midnight Baker

**Channel:** a cozy night-shift sourdough channel. **Prompt:** `/intro "Midnight Baker" --tagline "Fresh at 5 AM" --vibe "warm, cozy, steam rising from fresh bread"`

## Concept
Warm steam drifts up from the bottom of the frame and, as it clears, the channel name condenses out of it, like writing on a fogged bakery window. A bell rings when the name resolves.

## Decisions
- **Palette:** burnt-umber ground `#1a0f0a`, amber glow `#ffb454`, cream text `#fff1dc`. Warm and low-contrast everywhere except the name.
- **Type:** Playfair Display, mixed case. Friendly craft, not corporate.
- **Signature move:** 18 seeded steam wisps (blurred radial gradients) rise and dissolve; letters resolve from blur, left to right, as the steam passes.
- **Hit (2.4 s):** soft amber bloom behind the name and a bell.
- **Sound:** warm major pad, hissing steam swell, two bell partials, no drums.
