## Mixing rules
- Music at −14 LUFS-ish; SFX peaks ≤ −6 dBFS
- 150 ms fade in, 200 ms fade out
- Strongest transient lands exactly on the **Hit** beat
- With `--no-music`, keep SFX unless `--no-sfx` is set
