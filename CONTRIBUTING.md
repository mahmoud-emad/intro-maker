# Contributing

Thanks for helping! The highest-value contributions:

1. **Style presets**: add a section to `skills/intro/references/styles.md` (palette, type, motion, SFX) and an example in `examples/`.
2. **Audio**: CC0 / CC BY tracks and SFX, with licence + attribution in `skills/intro/references/audio.md`.
3. **Example intros**: a brief + rendered MP4/GIF under `examples/<name>/`.
4. **Bugs**: include your OS, Node version, FFmpeg version, `npx hyperframes doctor` output, and the `brief.md` that failed.

## Ground rules

- Keep intros short (≤ 8s) and legible.
- Only submit assets you have the right to redistribute.
- Keep `SKILL.md` lean; push detail into `references/`.
- One idea per PR.
